# Put your photo here

Save your portrait photo in this folder as:

    public/willaye.jpg

That single file powers:

- the hero "Meet Willaye" button (click it to see your face rebuilt as a live
  halftone dot sculpture that reacts to the cursor), and
- the halftone portrait in the About / CV section.

Tips for the best result:
- Use a well-lit photo with your face centred and a fairly plain background.
- Portrait orientation (taller than wide) works best — it is cropped to 4:5.
- Any size is fine; it is only read in the browser and never uploaded anywhere.

Prefer a different filename or location? Change `portrait` in
`src/content/site.ts` (set it to `""` to disable and show your initials
instead). Visitors can also always try their own photo with the hero
"Add your photo" button.
